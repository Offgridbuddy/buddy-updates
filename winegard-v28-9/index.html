What changed — and what did not (tap to open)
  • WHAT WAS SMOOTHED — three things, all done in the generator where the shape is defined (no mesh pushing — pushing the mesh was tried first and wrinkled the flat walls, so it was thrown out): (1) the gusset fairings are curved — the middle gusset's two straight taper wedges became curved lens fairings, and the rear gusset's straight scoop became a flowing curve that leaves the wall gently (about 5°) and steepens toward the tip; the middle gusset's sharp point (37° in plan) is now a blunt ~106° curve. (2) The puck's hard 90° step down onto the top plate is now an R5 fillet ring all the way around its foot, tangent to the plate and tangent to the puck wall. (3) The four concave roots where the front web block steps out of the channel walls got R2.5 fillets. The silhouette, the aero intent and the design's character are unchanged.
  • STRENGTH — NOTHING WAS REMOVED, measured by solid subtraction: v28.9 contains v28.8 entirely (material removed anywhere: 0.0003 mm³ — numerical dust, not a cut). v28.9 ADDS 2,607 mm³ (+1.2%): the curved fairings keep material the straight cuts took away (~2,026 mm³), and the fillets add ~581 mm³. Wall thickness is at or above v28.8 at every point of the part. The v28.5 thin-audit numbers are unchanged: plate join widths identical at all 11 measured stations (1.2 mm at first touch, full 6.4 mm behind it), thinnest plate columns identical, front tip 6.07 / 2.61 / 0.54 mm identical — and the v28.8 seam fix is intact: a fresh scan of the seam zone finds zero remaining gaps.
  • WHAT DID NOT MOVE — measured, not promised, with the same section method as the v28.8 proofs: cross-sections of v28.8 and v28.9 differ by 0.000 mm² outside the smoothing zones at all 21 stations, and every difference sits inside those zones. Channel inner walls, both cross-bolt holes (Ø6.350, identical voids), front face at y −7.400, the arch inner face at all four probed heights, and the wall intervals are all identical. Overall size identical on every axis. The tube cap is the same file, unchanged.
  • CONSIDERED AND LEFT ALONE — measured and rejected to keep fit and size exact: rounding the puck's top rim (it carries the part's highest point), rounding the top plate's rear corners (they carry the part's width), rounding the rear gusset's long diagonal (that diagonal IS the gusset — a true triangle by design; only its plan taper was smoothed), and rounding the plate's top rim edge (thickness risk at the rim).
